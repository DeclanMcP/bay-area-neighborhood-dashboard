    Get the daily top picks

    Every morning: the top areas for your profile, the best-scoring homes in them (each home scored on its own tract, its price vs the area's typical value, fit, and freshness), and what changed since yesterday. Register here and the picks come to your inbox. This page is static, so your registration is emailed to the project owner, who runs the daily send - no account, no tracking.

    Sources: Census ACS 2023 5-year and 2020 Census (PL), Zillow ZHVI, FHFA HPI, FEMA National Risk Index, CAASPP, SFPD / Oakland PD / Sonoma County Sheriff, CalEnviroScreen 4.0, CA FAIR Plan, Open-Meteo (ERA5), EPA Smart Location Database, US DOT BTS National Transportation Noise Map. Projections are backtested per dimension; methods, scores, and rejected alternatives live in the project repo (docs/PROJECTIONS.md, docs/DATA_SOURCES.md, docs/ADVERSARIAL_REVIEW.md).
