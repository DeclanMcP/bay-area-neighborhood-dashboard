Bay Area Neighborhood Dashboard

Every census tract in the nine-county Bay Area, measured on public data. Toggle to Projected for backtested 5- and 10-year projections.

Rankings

Young family with $500k saved. Score: schools level + momentum 35%, price performance (past 5y growth + projected) 25%, affordability 20%, safety 20% where a crime feed covers the tract (otherwise the other factors re-weight and the row is badged). Tracts are gated to $500k-$1.35M median value. Click a row to inspect that tract.

Dataset checks

Computed live from the loaded data file, not hard-coded.

    Sources: Census ACS 2023 5-year and 2020 Census (PL), Zillow ZHVI, FHFA HPI, FEMA National Risk Index, CAASPP, SFPD / Oakland PD / Sonoma County Sheriff, CalEnviroScreen 4.0, CA FAIR Plan, Open-Meteo (ERA5). Projections are backtested per dimension; methods, scores, and rejected alternatives live in the project repo (docs/PROJECTIONS.md, docs/DATA_SOURCES.md, docs/ADVERSARIAL_REVIEW.md).
